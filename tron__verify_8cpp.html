Detailed Description

Implementation of the local TransferContract field check.

Decodes the protobuf nesting raw_data.contract[0].parameter.value and reads owner_address, to_address and amount at their decoded positions. The contract must be a single TransferContract (type and Any.type_url both checked); unknown or repeated fields inside it are rejected, so bytes smuggled into another field or another contract type cannot pass.

Self-check:

g++ -DTRON_VERIFY_SELFTEST tron_verify.cpp -o tron_verify_selftest
./tron_verify_selftest
